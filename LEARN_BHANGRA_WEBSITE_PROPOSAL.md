# Learn Bhangra Website Proposal

This proposal describes a mobile-friendly website for Learn Bhangra, where visitors can find workshops, book tickets, and enjoy videos and photos from past events. The owner will be able to update workshop information and media without needing visitors to create accounts.

## Section 1: Website Pages

The website will have six main pages:

- **Home:** Introduces Learn Bhangra and highlights the next confirmed workshop, with links to booking, past workshops, and the story.
- **Our Story:** Shares the story of Learn Bhangra and introduces its instructors or community.
- **Workshops:** Lists upcoming and past workshops with dates, prices, locations, and booking details.
- **Past Workshops:** Shows videos and photos from previous workshops and choreography, with song names and other event details.
- **FAQs:** Answers common questions about workshops, what to wear, experience levels, and booking.
- **Contact:** Provides contact details and social media links.

## Section 2: Website Features

### Workshop dates and availability

Workshops take place every two weeks, although exact dates may change. The website will show the next confirmed workshop and will not display unconfirmed dates as final. When a date has not been announced, the page will say **“Next workshop date coming soon”** and invite visitors to sign up for email updates.

Visitors will be able to browse upcoming and past workshops. Each listing will show the date, price, location, booking link, and places remaining. With direct website checkout, availability will update as tickets are reserved and sold. If Humanitix is used instead, remaining places will depend on the availability information its platform supports.

### Email updates without visitor accounts

Visitors will not need to create an account or log in. They can choose to provide their email address to receive updates about upcoming workshops. The sign-up will explain what updates they will receive and provide an unsubscribe option.

### Private owner update area

The owner will have a private login to add, edit, and remove workshops, update prices and availability, and mark workshops as upcoming or past. The owner will also be able to upload videos and photos for visitors to view on the website.

### Past workshops and choreography

Visitors will be able to view approved videos and photos without logging in. Each entry will show the song name and other details provided by the owner, such as the workshop date or choreography name. Searching videos by song name is an optional enhancement for a later update; song names will still be displayed with the videos.

### Ticket booking

**Recommended for launch: sell tickets directly through the Learn Bhangra website.** Visitors will choose a workshop and pay on the website. The owner will manage workshop listings, ticket availability, and discounts through the private update area, keeping ticket sales under Learn Bhangra's control.

Humanitix can be used as an alternative if preferred. In that case, the website will link visitors to Humanitix to pay and receive their tickets, and any discounts will be set up through Humanitix using the features it supports.

Direct website sales have lower estimated transaction fees in the example below, but require a custom booking and payment system to be built and maintained.

### Example ticket fee comparison

This example uses the fee estimates provided: **1.75% + $0.30 per transaction** for website payments and **4% + $0.99 per paid ticket** for Humanitix. These are planning estimates; the providers' prices at the time of sign-up will apply.

Assumptions: a **$30** ticket, **one ticket per transaction**, **50 tickets sold at each workshop**, and workshops every two weeks. This gives an estimate of **26 workshops and 1,300 tickets per year**.

| Option | Fee on one $30 ticket | Fees for 1,300 tickets |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Website payment: 1.75% of $30 ($0.525) + $0.30 | $0.825, about $0.83 | $1,072.50 |
| Humanitix: 4% of $30 ($1.20) + $0.99 | $2.19 | $2,847.00 |

Under these assumptions, website payments would cost about **$1,774.50 less in ticket fees over the year**. The $0.30 website fee is charged per transaction, so it may be charged once when someone buys multiple tickets in one order. The comparison covers ticket fees; it does not include the extra cost of building and maintaining a website booking system.

## Section 3: Technical Requirements, Costs, and Timeline

### Technical requirements

- A registered domain name, such as a `.com.au` address. Domains are registered for a set period and renewed, rather than purchased permanently.
- Website hosting so visitors can access the website.
- A custom email address, such as `info@learnbhangra.com.au`, subject to domain availability.
- Secure online hosting for the owner update area, workshop information, and uploaded media.
- A private owner login. Visitors will not need to log in.

### Estimated ongoing costs

- **Domain:** Approximately **$45 for the first three years**, then **$21–$25 per year** for renewal. Actual prices depend on the domain and registrar.
- **Website hosting, custom email, and owner update area hosting:** Estimated together at **$80–$85 per month**, or **$960–$1,020 per year**.
- **Ticket fees:** Depend on the booking option and the number and price of tickets sold. The example above compares the estimated fees.

### Timeline

Delivery is estimated at **30–45 days** after the requirements are approved and the owner has provided the workshop details, photos, videos, song names, and other website content. This estimate includes multiple design and review iterations and end-to-end testing before launch.

**Branding suggestion:** As the brand grows, using **Learn Bhangra** as the main name may help it reach people beyond Melbourne. **Learn Bhangra Melbourne** can still describe the local community and its workshops. This is simply a suggestion to consider as the brand develops.
